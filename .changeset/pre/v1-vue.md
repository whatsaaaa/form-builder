1.0. From here the package follows semantic versioning: a patch fixes bugs, a minor adds, and only a major breaks. What that covers, including the parts of Formisch the adapter reads outside Formisch's own semver, is in the stability policy, https://whatsaaaa.github.io/form-builder/guide/stability, and the versions it supports are in https://whatsaaaa.github.io/form-builder/guide/support. Its peer `@whatsaaaa/form-builder-schema` is now 1.x: update the two together. Coming from 0.x, every change on the way to 1.0 and how to update is in https://whatsaaaa.github.io/form-builder/guide/migrating-to-1.0.
