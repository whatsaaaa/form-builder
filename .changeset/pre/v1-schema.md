1.0. From here the package follows semantic versioning: a patch fixes bugs, a minor adds, and only a major breaks. What that covers is in the stability policy, https://whatsaaaa.github.io/form-builder/guide/stability, and the versions of Valibot, Formisch and TypeScript it supports are in https://whatsaaaa.github.io/form-builder/guide/support. Definitions keep `version: "1"` for all of 1.x. Coming from 0.x, every change on the way to 1.0 and how to update is in https://whatsaaaa.github.io/form-builder/guide/migrating-to-1.0.
